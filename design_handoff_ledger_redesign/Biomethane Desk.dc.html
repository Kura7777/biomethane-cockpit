EU directive · consolidated to August 2026

RED III Directive (EU) 2023/2413

Directive of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Directive (EU) 2018/2001 as regards the promotion of energy from renewable sources · Articles 25, 29, 30, 31, 31a & Annex IX

Golden trading desk rule

A consignment is only tradeable into a compliance mechanism if the certification scheme is recognised for that mechanism, the volume can evidence Union Database ingestion, and the chain of custody is mass balance. Book & claim satisfies none of the three, whatever the physical molecule did.

Statutory framework

Article 29 sets the sustainability and greenhouse-gas saving criteria; Article 30 fixes the chain-of-custody rules under which mass balance is mandatory for compliance claims; Article 31a establishes the Union Database and the obligation to register every transaction in it.

Art. 29(10) Minimum GHG saving of 65% for transport biofuels from installations commissioned after 2021.
Art. 30(1) Mass balance system required for verification of sustainability and GHG criteria.
Art. 31a Union Database registration of all renewable fuel transactions, with grid-injected gas treated per Reg. (EU) 2024/2792.
Annex IX Part A Advanced feedstock list, including manure and slurry, eligible for the advanced sub-target.
