## IT — obligation to put into consumption (D.Lgs. 199/2021 art. 39; DM 16 Mar 2023 n.107; biomethane CIC under DM 2 Mar 2018 as amended)
### Facts
- Obligated parties are those putting petrol, diesel, methane into consumption for road/rail transport; "immissione in consumo" is inferred from excise payability | https://www.certifico.com/id/20787 (mirror of DM 107/2023, saved _pdf\dm107.txt) art. 2(1)(v), art. 2(2) | "soggetti obbligati: i soggetti che immettono in consumo vettori energetici rinnovabili di origine biologica, benzina, gasolio, metano, per i trasporti stradali e ferroviari" ; "l'immissione in consumo ... è desunta dal verificarsi dei presupposti per il pagamento dell'accisa" (put into consumption = excise chargeable) | SECONDARY (mirror of official text)
- Biomethane CIC are not issued under DM 107 itself but under DM 2 Mar 2018 | DM 107 art. 6(3) | "L'immissione in consumo di biometano dà diritto a ricevere i certificati secondo le prescrizioni ed i requisiti previsti dal decreto del D.M. 2 marzo 2018." | SECONDARY (mirror)
- Under DM 2018 art. 5(1) the CIC for biomethane are issued to the PRODUCER of biomethane injected in the Italian gas network and used for transport in Italian territory, backed by supply contracts/invoices with Italian road filling stations | https://www.mimit.gov.it/images/stories/normativa/DM-biometano-2-marzo_2018_FINALE.pdf (saved _pdf\dm2018.txt) art. 5(1),(2) | "Al produttore di biometano immesso nella rete del gas naturale ed utilizzato per i trasporti nel territorio italiano vengono rilasciati un numero di certificati di immissione in consumo di biocarburanti" ; GSE acquires "i contratti di fornitura di gas naturale e biometano e le relative fatturazioni ... con soggetti titolari di impianti di distribuzione stradale ed autostradale" | PRIMARY
- CIC so issued are usable only by obligated parties (art. 5(7)); the 'GSE withdrawal' mechanism of art. 6 (advanced biomethane) passes CIC to obligated parties paying the cost | same, art. 5(7), art. 6(1)(c) | PRIMARY
- The Italian biomethane GO (art. 4) is for biomethane NOT accessing the transport/other support provisions and is "senza destinazione specifica di uso" | same art. 4(1) | "L'emissione della garanzia di origine è ammessa solo per il biometano ... che non acceda ad altre disposizioni di cui al presente decreto" | PRIMARY
- ONLY cross-border door in the decree: plants located in other Member States (and bordering FTA third countries) that "physically export" their biomethane to Italy may take part in art. 6 (GSE withdrawal of advanced biomethane) IF (a) a cooperation agreement exists with that State under arts. 5-10 or 11 of Directive 2009/28/CE, (b) the agreement establishes reciprocity, (c) plant meets all the subjective/objective requirements of Italian plants; volume capped by formula QUE (Italian-imported gas share x Eurostat biomethane share); GSE verifies annually by 1 Dec | same, art. 12(1)-(4) | "Gli impianti ubicati sul territorio di altri Stati membri ... che esportano fisicamente la loro produzione di biometano in Italia possono partecipare ... a condizione che: a) esista un accordo con lo Stato Membro ... redatto ai sensi degli articoli da 5 a 10 o dell'articolo 11 della direttiva 2009/28/CE; b) l'accordo stabilisca un sistema di reciprocità" | PRIMARY
- Same conditions slideshow summary: "Contratti di trasporto dall'estero fino al PSV" (transport contracts from abroad to the PSV required) | https://www.federmetano.it/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/Biometano-e-Biocarburanti-avanzati-il-nuovo-Sistema-incentivante-.pdf | SECONDARY
- DM 107 art. 6(3),(16),(17): liquefied biomethane in pure form put into consumption at a liquefaction plant DIRECTLY connected to the production plant earns CIC to the obligated party, with a sustainability certificate; no further operating incentive | DM 107 art. 6 | PRIMARY-mirror. Domestic production-site scenario, not a grid mass-balance import.
- Italian transport-plant GOs cannot be exported (pass 1 / registry-hub file); GSE is on the AIB gas hub.
### Verdicts
- Mass-balance PoS import (foreign injection, delivered via grid, no Italian CIC claim): NOT POSSIBLE for the CIC obligation on the written rules. CIC arise (i) to Italian-injected biomethane producers, or (ii) via art. 12 for foreign plants only under a Directive 2009/28/CE cooperation agreement with reciprocity (INFERENCE: no such agreement located; none found in search, see below). Even then it is a GSE-withdrawal route with a capped quantity, not a free PoS market.
- Residual uncertainty: the DM 107 mirror has no text on foreign-injected biomethane, and the GSE Procedure applicative (not downloadable, 403) could contain implementing detail; art. 12 is dated 2018 and may have been amended by the 2022 DM 340 (not read). Status: NOT POSSIBLE on primary text; check the GSE for amendments.

### Verdicts
- TIRUERT 2026: NOT POSSIBLE for biomethane of any origin (bioGNV is not part of TIRUERT in 2026; the only integration clause was deleted; petrol/diesel streams only).
- IRICC from 2027: OPEN. The draft counts biogas via GOs held by the distributor plus a sustainability proof and is not origin-restricted on its face (it names equivalent foreign support schemes, implying foreign biogas may count); the draft is not law and says nothing explicit on imported injection or on whether the GO must be French/AIB-imported.
- CPB (heat): NOT POSSIBLE (French injection only).

## EE — national transport-sector target accounting (Liquid Fuel Act; Energy Sector Organisation Act; Elering biomethane register and "transport statistics")
### Facts
- Estonian target accounting for biomethane in transport is GO-based, with sustainability data on the GO as a prerequisite | AIB Domain Protocol Estonia (Elering), clean version 20241210, https://www.aib-net.org/facts/aib-member-countries-regions/domain-protocols (saved _pdf\AIB-2024-DPEE-_Domain_Protocol_Elering_Estonia_Clean_version_20241210.txt) C.3.4.3 | "biomethane consumption reporting for national transport sector target accounting is based on GOs ... Inclusion of information on sustainability criteria compliance of biomethane on the respective GOs is a prerequisite for national GO-based biomethane consumption target accounting solutions." | PRIMARY
- Transport certificates are issued to the gas supplier only when a biomethane GO is cancelled against transport consumption; liquid fuel suppliers then use them for the national obligation | same C.4.3, C.6.3 | "When a biomethane guarantee of origin is cancelled against transport sector consumption, biomethane transport sector certificates are issued to the gas supplier. The certificates can be used by liquid fuel suppliers to fulfill the national transport sector obligations." | PRIMARY
- Imported GOs cannot automatically be used for the obligation | same E.10.3 | "Imported biomethane GOs can be used to provide proof of biomethane consumption to the final customer (disclosure purpose) but cannot automatically be used for fulfilling the national renewable energy obligations (target accounting purpose)." | PRIMARY
- Imported GOs must not come from supported production | same C.4.7 | "Renewable energy that corresponds to the imported guarantees of origin shall not have received support via national public support schemes." | PRIMARY
- No ex-domain cancellation | same C.3.6.3 | "Cancellation for usage in another Domain (i.e. Ex-Domain Cancellations) is not allowed." | PRIMARY

### Verdicts
- Pure PoS mass-balance delivery without an Estonian GO: NOT POSSIBLE (accounting is GO-based; certificates arise from GO cancellation against transport).
- Imported GO (AIB) + PoS: not automatic ("cannot automatically be used"); whether Elering/Environmental Board accept it case by case = OPEN.

### Verdicts
- Pure PoS mass balance with NO GO: NOT POSSIBLE (DAEI is issued against used GOs).
- EU-origin biomethane: POSSIBLE via GO + PoS. Conditions: (1) GO imported via AIB hub / cancelled for Lithuania with that annotation; (2) mass balance applied; (3) PoS/sustainability data and GHG intensity on the GO (point 16); (4) injection and withdrawal points connected by the gas transport system; (5) not counted toward the origin Member State's RES target (double count declaration); (6) filling-point and supplier data; (7) Amber Grid approval. Origins: any Member State whose registry transfers via the AIB hub (LT is hub-connected) and whose grid is interconnected with Lithuania (via Poland GIPL, Latvia, Finland/Estonia via Balticconnector). Whether RED III reform (Jun 2026) changed these conditions: OPEN.
