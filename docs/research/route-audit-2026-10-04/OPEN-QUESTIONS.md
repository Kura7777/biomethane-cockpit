## 1. Questions Already Answered by Synthesis & Primary Fact-Checks

The following open questions identified in preliminary research files were formally settled by `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` and primary statutory source verification. They are removed from the active enquiry list:

1. **VertiCer (NL) ERGaR Connection Status**:
   - *Preliminary Question*: Is VertiCer's ERGaR connection fully switched off, and was any date after 1 Jul 2026 used?
   - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §1 citing VertiCer FAQ (13 Aug 2025 / live FAQ): VertiCer officially exited ERGaR on 1 July 2026 pursuant to Ministry instructions. All ERGaR export and import channels to/from the Netherlands (including dena Germany) are permanently closed.
2. **E-Control (AT) AIB Hub Reconnection**:
   - *Preliminary Question*: Is the E-Control gas HKN database reconnected to the AIB Hub following the planned maintenance window?
   - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §5 citing AIB news (22 Apr 2026) and AIB Hub Transfer Flows dataset: the planned outage ran from 30 April to 17 May 2026; live transfers resumed in May 2026, and AIB data confirms completed Austrian gas flows through August 2026.
3. **Sweden (SE) AIB Gas Scheme Hub Connection**:
   - *Preliminary Question*: Has Sweden joined the AIB Gas Scheme Group and is Cesar connected to the AIB Gas Hub?
   - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §5 citing AIB news (20 Aug 2026): AIB Gas Scheme Group formally approved the Swedish Energy Agency application on 20 August 2026; Swedish EECS gas GOs are tradeable over the AIB Hub from 1 September 2026.
4. **German THG-Quote (DE) Cross-Border Mass Balance**:
   - *Preliminary Question*: Does German law accept biomethane injected elsewhere in the EU for THG-Quote compliance via grid mass balance?
   - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §4 citing the *Zweites Gesetz zur Weiterentwicklung der THG-Quote*, BImSchG §37b(6) n.F. (Bundestag adopted 23 April 2026, Bundesrat approved 8 May 2026): grid gas counts as biomethane if matched by biomethane injected anywhere in the EU excise territory.
5. **Italian CIC (IT) Foreign Injection Eligibility**:
   - *Preliminary Question*: Can biomethane injected abroad earn Italian CIC credits under DM 2 Mar 2018 or DM 107/2023?
   - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §6 citing DM 2 Mar 2018 Art. 5(1) and Art. 12: CIC are restricted to biomethane injected into the Italian network. Foreign plants require an active Directive 2009/28 cooperation agreement with reciprocity, and no such agreement exists.
6. **French TIRUERT 2026 (FR) Biomethane Eligibility**:
   - *Preliminary Question*: Is biomethane (bioGNV) included in the TIRUERT transport tax scheme for 2026?
   - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §6 citing Assemblée Nationale Amendment 3492 (Government, "Adopté"): deleted Art. 16 ter that would have added bioGNV to TIRUERT; TIRUERT 2026 covers only petrol and diesel.
7. **Dutch ERE (NL) Foreign Green Gas GvO Eligibility**:
   - *Preliminary Question*: Can foreign green gas GOs be used for inboekingen in the Dutch transport quota (ERE)?
   - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §4 citing NEa FAQ and Regeling energie vervoer Art. 7: only green gas GvOs relating to biogas produced in the Netherlands can be used for inboekingen.
8. **Czech Transport Obligation (CZ) PoS vs GO Requirement**:
   - *Preliminary Question*: Does Czech Act No. 165/2012 Coll. accept foreign biomethane via a PoS alone without a GO?
   - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §3 citing Act No. 165/2012 Coll. §47d(3)(a)1: compliance can be proven by a GO or another proof of sustainability (PoS), with no origin restriction.
9. **Swiss Fuel Tax Relief (CH) Grid Mass Balance**:
   - *Preliminary Question*: Can biomethane delivered through the pipeline grid claim Swiss mineral oil tax (MinöStG) relief?
   - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §4 citing Federal Council reports (2015 & 2023): gas delivered through the pipeline grid is classified as natural gas at customs and is excluded from tax relief or CO2 levy exemptions.
10. **Norwegian Sales Mandate (NO) Biogas Eligibility**:
    - *Preliminary Question*: Does the Norwegian biofuel sales mandate (omsetningskrav) count biomethane?
    - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §4 citing Miljødirektoratet: "Biogass er ikke omfattet av omsetningskravene" (mandate applies exclusively to liquid biofuels).
11. **UK RTFO (GB) European Pipeline Biomethane Eligibility**:
    - *Preliminary Question*: Does the UK RTFO accept biomethane injected in continental Europe and delivered by pipeline under mass balance?
    - *Fact That Answered It*: `SYNTHESIS-NOTES.md` §2 citing DfT RTFO Biomethane Guidance (Dec 2024) §3.17 & §2.13: EU pipeline biomethane is accepted for RTFCs provided there is an interconnected pipeline route and cross-border capacity is booked and nominated.

### 1. European Commission — DG ENER (Decarbonisation and Energy Sustainability)
- **Country**: European Union (Brussels, Belgium)
- **Contact**: `ener-biocarburants@ec.europa.eu`, `https://energy.ec.europa.eu/contact_en`
- **Total Cells Settled**: 69 cells

#### `Q-EU-1`: Third-Country Grid Mass Balance & UDB Integration
- **Question**: Will the implementing rules under Directive (EU) 2023/2413 (RED III) Art. 31a and the revised Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/996 recognize gas grids of physically connected third countries (specifically Great Britain, Switzerland, and pipeline transit to Ireland via Great Britain) as part of the single mass-balance system under the Union Database (UDB)?
- **Cells Settled**: 69 cells.
  - Settles GB as origin into all 23 connected EU destination countries (e.g. `GB>DE`, `GB>SE`, `GB>CZ`, `GB>DK`).
  - Settles CH as origin into all 23 connected EU destination countries (e.g. `CH>DE`, `CH>SE`, `CH>CZ`).
  - Settles continental EU origins delivering by pipeline into Ireland via Moffat (`23 EU origins > IE`).
- **Why Public Sources Didn't Settle It**: An informal Commission e-mail of 30 Oct 2023 stated that third countries cannot be part of the UDB mass-balancing facility. However, RED III Art. 31a(2) refers broadly to "the interconnected gas system" without explicit third-country statutory exclusion, and industry groups (Eurogas joint statement 28 May 2026) have formally petitioned the Commission for an interim regulatory solution.

### 2. DGEC / EEX — Direction Générale de l'Énergie et du Climat & European Energy Exchange
- **Country**: France

- **Total Cells Settled**: 68 cells

#### `Q-FR-1`: Mandatory French ETS/ESR Tag Acceptance on AIB Gas GOs
- **Question**: Does EEX accept imported EECS gas GOs from all AIB Gas Scheme members provided the mandatory ETS/ESR eligibility tag is populated, and does EEX publish an approved list of registries capable of transmitting this tag?
- **Cells Settled**: 14 incoming GO cells into France (`CZ>FR`, `EE>FR`, `ES>FR`, `FI>FR`, `IT>FR`, `LT>FR`, `LV>FR`, `PT>FR`, `SE>FR`, `SK>FR`, `HU>FR`, `BE>FR`, `AT>FR`, `NL>FR`).

#### `Q-FR-2`: Cross-Border Biomethane Eligibility Under IRICC (Post-2026)
- **Question**: Under the *Incitation à la réduction de l'intensité carbone des carburants* (IRICC) mechanism entering into force on 1 January 2027 (Code de l'énergie L. 295-1 ff.), will fuel distributors be permitted to count biomethane injected in another EU Member State and delivered by grid mass balance, evidenced by an imported EECS GO (registered in EEX) and an ISCC/REDcert PoS?

- **Why Public Sources Didn't Settle It**: The May 2025 consultation draft for IRICC is origin-neutral and mentions equivalent foreign schemes (Art. L. 295-3 4°), but the final promulgated decrees and implementing rules for cross-border grid imports have not been published.

#### `Q-FR-3`: Export of PoS on Biomethane from Purchase Obligation Plants
- **Question**: May biomethane produced by a French plant under an *obligation d'achat* or *complément de rémunération* contract be exported with an ISCC EU/REDcert PoS via grid mass balance to a buyer in another Member State, given that the statutory GO is owned and auctioned by the French State?
- **Cells Settled**: 27 outgoing PoS cells from France (`FR > all 27 destinations`).
- **Why Public Sources Didn't Settle It**: French Energy Code Art. L. 314-14 and EEX DP establish that GOs for subsidized gas are issued to the State. However, whether the physical gas with an ISCC mass-balance PoS can be sold cross-border without double counting or subsidy clawback is not settled in published texts.
