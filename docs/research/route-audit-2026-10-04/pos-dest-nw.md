### Verdicts
- THG-Quote: EU-injected biomethane delivered by mass balance = POSSIBLE under the 2026 amendment (EU origins any; third countries only if grid physically connected + UDB). Practical proof route before UDB gas module (Nabisy/ISCC/REDcert) = OPEN. Subsidised-in-origin volumes = OPEN/RISK (text silent, political intent to exclude; matters for DK/NL/FR/IT supported gas).
- BEHG/EBeV, EU-ETS, GEG/BEG: POSSIBLE for foreign-injected mass-balance gas (dated 2023 sources). ETS also needs same-grid link (see registry-hub doc).
- EEG: NOT POSSIBLE.

## NL — ERE (Energie voor Vervoer) and planned Bijmengverplichting groen gas
### Facts
- ERE grid gas: an inboeker may only "green" gas delivered to transport via the grid with Dutch-produced green gas GvOs. | https://www.emissieautoriteit.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/faq-he/kan-ik-groen-gas-gvos-uit-het-buitenland-gebruiken-voor-het-inboeken-van-biogas | "Nee, dat kan niet. Alleen Garanties van Oorsprong (GvO's) voor groen gas die betrekking hebben op in Nederland geproduceerd biogas kunnen gebruikt worden voor inboekingen (Regeling energie vervoer, artikel 7)" | PRIMARY (NEa FAQ)
- Subsidised gas excluded from ERE; GvO must show no subsidy. | https://internetconsultatie.nl/redii_regeling_energie_vervoer_kalenderjaren_2022_2030/document/7278 (toelichting art.7, 2021 text) | "De garanties van oorsprong voor gas uit hernieuwbare bronnen moeten weergeven dat geen subsidie voor de productie is ontvangen" | PRIMARY (2021 explanatory note; rule may have been renumbered)
- Draft REV2026 implementation (RED III) would delete the administrative "greening" of LNG with GvOs. | https://internetconsultatie.nl/rev2026_implementatie_rediii/document/14551 | "De mogelijkheid tot het inboeken van geleverd vloeibaar gemaakt aardgas (LNG), ... boekhoudkundig vergroend met garanties van oorsprong (GvO's) ... wordt geschrapt." | PRIMARY (draft)
- NEW (pending): Wet bijmengverplichting groen gas (Wet milieubeheer titel 9.10, Kamerstuk 36947), gas suppliers to ETS2 end users from 2027. NEa: "De verplichting voor de CO₂-ketenemissiereductie start in 2027" (https://www.emissieautoriteit.nl/regelgeving/bijmengverplichting-groen-gas/wat-is-de-bijmengverplichting-groen-gas)
- Foreign gas allowed in the bill: | https://www.emissieautoriteit.nl/regelgeving/bijmengverplichting-groen-gas/wat-is-de-bijmengverplichting-groen-gas | "Dat gas kan geproduceerd zijn in Nederland of in andere EU-landen, mits het aan de geldende eisen voor voldoet." ; "Groen gas waarvoor exploitatiesubsidie is ontvangen (zoals de SDE++ in Nederland) is uitgesloten." | PRIMARY
- Mechanism = GvO (via AIB hub into VertiCer) + PoS for the same quantity, swapped for a GGE; not pure mass balance. | Staatscourant 2026, 19452 (Nader rapport, 4 Jun 2026) https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2026-19452.pdf | "Een combinatie van een GvO en PoS, zolang deze zijn uitgegeven voor dezelfde hoeveelheid gas, kan door leveranciers bij de NEa omgeruild worden voor een GGE." ; "GvO's uit andere lidstaten dienen via de Association of Issuing Bodies (AIB) ingevoerd te worden bij VertiCer." ; "Producenten van buitenlands groen gas kunnen hiertoe een verzoek indienen bij de nationale autoriteit die in dat land belast is met de uitgifte van GvO's." | PRIMARY
- Foreign gas was first excluded, then admitted after the European Commission's detailed opinion (TRIS); Commission still has questions. | same Staatscourant | "buitenlands groen gas niet langer is uitgesloten van de bijmengverplichting. Toch resteren ... bij de Europese Commissie nog vragen" | PRIMARY
- Counting note: physical import counts for NL ESR figures. | same | "Als het groen gas dat in een andere lidstaat is geproduceerd naar Nederland wordt getransporteerd ten behoeve van de invoeding in het Nederlandse gasnet, dan tellen de emissiereductiecijfers in Nederland mee." | PRIMARY
### Verdicts
- ERE (transport, grid CNG): foreign-injected gas NOT POSSIBLE via GvO route (only NL-produced GvOs). Physical import by truck/LNG with PoS (liquid/bio-LNG route) not examined here.
- Bijmengverplichting groen gas: POSSIBLE IN PRINCIPLE for gas from other EU states, but needs (GvO via AIB) + PoS, so origin must be on the AIB gas hub (AT, BE-Brussels, CZ, EE, FI, FR, HU, IT, LV, LT, PT, SK, ES, SE, DE? (DE is ERGaR-only: route unclear = OPEN), DK (AIB gas pending = not yet)). Bill not yet law; subsidised volumes excluded. Rules (AMvB) not yet written.

## FI — Distribution obligation (jakeluvelvoite, Laki uusiutuvien polttoaineiden käytön edistämisestä liikenteessä 446/2007), biogas excise tax, ETS
### Facts
- Biogas has been inside the road-transport distribution obligation since 2022; Energiavirasto administers it. | https://tem.fi/-/biopolttoaineet-jakeluvelvoitteeseen ; search extract of Energiavirasto/TEM texts | "Biokaasu otettiin tieliikenteen biopolttoaineiden jakeluvelvoitteen piiriin vuonna 2022." | SECONDARY (search summary; official guide https://energiavirasto.fi/documents/11120570/103079467/Jakeluvelvoiteohje.pdf could not be fetched: Cloudflare 403)

- Same deck, emissions trading and excise table: PoS required; biogas "injected into the common gas network in the EU area"; GO cancelled for the same quantity and batch required. | same slide 44 | "Biogas is injected into the common gas network in the EU area" ; "Canceled GOs must be issued for the same sustainable biogas quantity and batch." | SECONDARY
- Sustainability proof: national system for Finland; for transfers abroad a Commission-approved voluntary scheme is required; GO data must be recorded in UDB. | same slides 10, 12 | "For transfers abroad, a voluntary system approved by the European Union is required." | SECONDARY
- Imports in practice are GO-based: 450 GWh GOs imported 2024, mostly from Denmark. | https://gasgrid.fi/en/bulletins/the-import-of-biogas-from-europe-to-finland-increased-significantly-last-year-and-growth-continues/ | "Most of the gas GOs imported to Finland last year came from Denmark." ; "Only guarantees of origin can verify that gas has been produced from renewable sources." | PRIMARY (Gasgrid = GO issuer/TSO)
### Verdicts
- Excise tax + ETS: gas injected in another EU-grid state and delivered by mass balance: POSSIBLE per Gasgrid summary (needs PoS + GO + same physically connected network; Finland is linked to EE/LV/LT/PL via Balticconnector, INFERENCE from the "common gas network in the EU area" wording). Origin must be physically connected: all EU gas-grid states in the continental system are connected; non-connected origin = not possible by mass balance.
- Distribution obligation (transport): imported/mass-balance gas = OPEN (no primary text on cross-border gas obtained).

## GB — Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO Order 2007 as amended; DfT RTFO guidance)
### Facts
- DfT's RTFO biomethane guidance explicitly allows European biomethane injected abroad and moved to GB by interconnected pipeline under mass balance. | https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6758544782c7cd4258eb64a8/rtfo-biomethane-guidance.pdf ("Valid from 01/01/23", republished Dec 2024; saved rtfobm2.txt) para 3.17 | "For biomethane imported from Europe via transmission and distribution pipeline infrastructure, it must be demonstrated that there is an interconnected pipeline route from the point of production in the country of origin to the UK pipeline grid, such that the supply of biomethane going into the grid could have been in contact with the gas being extracted." | PRIMARY
- Each balancing zone is a separate mass-balance system, so each cross-border point must carry the flow in the claimed direction. | same, paras 2.9-2.10 and 3.18 | "each pipeline grid or balancing area/zone is treated as a separate mass balance `site' or `system'" ; "Commercial reverse flow at cross border interconnection points between the interconnected gas pipeline grids of the UK and Europe is permitted ... Reverse flow capacity, where it is needed in order to `flow' gas towards the UK from Europe, can and should be booked (and reverse flows nominated) separately by shippers" | PRIMARY
- Same principles applied beyond Europe ("Elsewhere in the world ... there must be a continuous interconnected pipeline route"): no EU-only limit. | same para 3.17, 1.4 | PRIMARY
- GOs and green gas certificates cannot support RTFCs; certificates must stay with the gas and be retired by the claimant. | same para 3.5 | "Renewable guarantees of origin and other commercial green gas certificates are not acceptable to support an application for RTFCs." | PRIMARY
- Sustainability: PoS from a recognised voluntary scheme or full-chain evidence; duty paid in GB (RTFC quantity <= HMRC duty-paid quantity). | same paras 3.4, 3.6 | PRIMARY
- Multiple-incentive rule: fuel supported under any UK or international production support scheme (feed-in tariffs, premiums, renewable fuel obligations) is ineligible; investment aid and tax relief are not counted as support. | https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/69a80c033b5b78231d1a9b78/dft-rtfo-compliance-guidance-26.pdf (Jan 2026) para 5.3-5.4 | "must not ... be: counted under a UK renewable energy obligation other than the RTFO; counted under any other support scheme of the UK, or internationally that benefits the end supply of fuel such as feed-in tariffs or premium payments" | PRIMARY
- Northern Ireland is covered by the RTFO; GB, NI, IE are separate balancing areas. | biomethane guidance 2.8, 2.11 | PRIMARY
### Verdicts
- RTFO: biomethane injected in another country and delivered by mass balance through the interconnected grid: POSSIBLE in principle for any origin with an unbroken interconnected pipeline route and nominated flow in the GB direction (any EU/EEA country, not EU-only). Excludes volumes that receive production support (feed-in tariff/premium; DK, DE EEG, NL SDE++, FR FIT, IT, etc.); GOs not accepted. Practical risk: physical flows on IUK/BBL are mostly GB to continent; reverse flow must be booked and nominated (INFERENCE on commercial feasibility; note GB was a net exporter in many periods).
