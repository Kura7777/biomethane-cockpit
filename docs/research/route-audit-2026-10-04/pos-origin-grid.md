Such guarantees of origin, once transferred, shall not be tradable outside the Union database." | PRIMARY

- State-aid layer (Landwärme case) | OIES NG203 | "EU state aid assessments must now pay closer attention to the cross-border cumulation of public support" ; "some Member States allow economic operators to opt out of national support schemes or require the repayment of subsidies when biomethane is exported" | SECONDARY
- Law-firm view | Dentons, 1 Apr 2026, https://www.dentons.com/en/insights/newsletters/2026/april/1/future-of-energy-markets-europe/challenges-affecting-the-development-of-a-pan-eu-biomethane-market (dentons.txt) | "Most biomethane today is produced under national schemes that prohibit the export of 'renewable value' to other Member States." ; "Spain, Italy, Poland: No documented export prohibitions comparable to France's." (the Italy statement is contradicted by the AIB Italian protocol, see IT) | SECONDARY
### Verdicts
- Nothing in RED III or IR 2022/996 bars a producer moving PoS with gas to another Member State; the EU interconnected system is one mass-balance unit. Restrictions come from (a) national support-scheme terms, (b) destination schemes, (c) the UDB (once live), which records support type per consignment.
- Third-country grids (GB, CH, NO, UA): NOT inside the single mass-balance system on the Commission's reading (SECONDARY evidence; no primary Commission text on this retrieved). Art. 31a itself says "the interconnected gas system" without limiting it to the EU; the EU-only reading rests on Commission statements. INFERENCE.
